    • Team

    The event is run by This Next Thing CLG, a non-profit organisation headed up by Abby, Eamon, and Paul.

    While the three of us oversee the main work, we highly encourage and welcome our community members to help out, and have listed some ways to do so here.

    Abigail Phoenix
    Executive Director
    Abby is a longtime conference organizer whose enthusiasm for community and connection is rivaled only by her love of the almighty spreadsheet. Her previous events include RailsConf, RubyConf, and Codeland.

    Eamon Leonard
    Commercial Director
    Eamon is a founding parter of Broadstone, a pre-seed investment firm based in Dublin. He has spent most of his career in early stage startupland developing and building products, leading teams, and creating communities.

    Paul Campbell
    Technical Director
    Paul is the co-founder and CEO of Team Tito. Paul presides over the making of Tito, an app for selling tickets online, and Vito, an app for building online communities. Paul also created Úll, a series of conferences inspired by Apple.
